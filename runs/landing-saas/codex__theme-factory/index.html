A FEW MORE THINGS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Getting your on-call in order should feel straightforward. We're here to help.

Get in touch
Can I import my existing schedule from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload your schedule as a CSV, match the columns to your team members and shift times, and review the import before it goes live.

What happens if nobody acknowledges an alert?

Rotaline follows your escalation policy, trying the next channel or person after the time you set. You can add a final team-wide notification so an unanswered alert stays visible.

Do you charge for SMS and phone calls?

SMS and phone alerts are included in Team and Business under fair-use limits. You'll see your usage in your workspace and be notified before any additional charges apply.

Can contractors or people outside my company be on a rotation?

Yes. Invite them by email and add them to a rotation just like anyone else. They count toward your plan's user total, and you control which services they can access.

How does the free trial work?

Try Team or Business for 14 days with no credit card required. At the end, choose a paid plan or keep using Free if your team fits its limits.
