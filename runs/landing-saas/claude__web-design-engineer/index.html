Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Contact us and a person on our team will reply.

Can I import my existing schedule from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV or paste rows straight from your spreadsheet, and Rotaline matches columns to people, dates and shift times. You review the imported rotation before anyone is notified.

What happens if nobody acknowledges an alert?

The alert moves to the next step of the service's escalation policy, for example from the primary to the secondary to the team lead. If every step is exhausted, the policy repeats and the incident is flagged in the next handoff note.

Do you charge for SMS and phone calls?

No. SMS and phone-call alerts are included in the per-user price of the Team and Business plans. The Free plan sends push and email alerts.

Can contractors or people outside my company be on a rotation?

Yes. Invite them by email as guests: they can take shifts, receive alerts and write handoff notes, but they only see the rotations they belong to. Guests count as users on paid plans.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of the Team plan with no credit card required. When the trial ends, choose a paid plan or move to Free; your rotations and history are kept either way.
