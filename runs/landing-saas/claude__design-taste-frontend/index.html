Questions, answered

Can I import my existing schedule from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV or paste rows from Google Sheets or Excel, and Rotaline matches names to your team and turns the dates into shifts. You review the result before anything is saved.

What happens if nobody acknowledges an alert?

The alert moves to the next step of the escalation policy after the delay you set, by SMS, a phone call or the next person. If every step runs out, the policy starts again and the team lead is told.

Do you charge for SMS and phone calls?

No. SMS and phone alerts are included in the Team and Business price, with no per-message fees. The Free plan sends push and email alerts only.

Can contractors or people outside my company be on a rotation?

Yes. Invite anyone by email address. They count as a user on your plan, and you can limit them to the rotations and services they cover.

How does the free trial work?

You get every Team feature free for 14 days, with no credit card. At the end, choose a plan, or your workspace moves to the Free plan and keeps your first rotation.
