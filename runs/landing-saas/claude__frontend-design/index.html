Questions before you switch

Something else on your mind? Email help@rotaline.example and a person on our support rotation will reply.

Can I import my existing schedule from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV or paste rows from Google Sheets or Excel, and Rotaline matches names and dates to a rotation. You can check every shift before the schedule goes live.

What happens if nobody acknowledges an alert?

The alert moves to the next step of your escalation policy, such as the secondary or the team lead, after the wait time you set. If it reaches the last step, Rotaline repeats the policy until someone acknowledges.

Do you charge for SMS and phone calls?

No. SMS and phone alerts are included in the Team and Business plans with no per-message fees, in every country we support.

Can contractors or people outside my company be on a rotation?

Yes. Invite anyone with an email address as a guest. Guests count as users on your plan and can only see the rotations they're part of.

How does the free trial work?

You get every Business feature for 14 days, and you don't need a credit card to start. When the trial ends, pick a plan or move to Free. Your schedules are kept either way.
