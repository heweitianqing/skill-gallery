Features

Everything on-call needs, without the spreadsheet

Rotaline keeps the schedule, the alerts and the handoff in one place, so nobody has to scroll back through a chat thread to find out who has the pager.

Rotation builder

Set up daily, weekly or follow-the-sun rotations across time zones. Rotaline handles daylight saving and uneven team sizes for you.

Swaps and overrides

Request a swap in two clicks. The team lead approves it straight from email or chat, and the schedule updates for everyone.

Escalation policies

Multi-step escalation by push notification, SMS and phone call. If the first responder doesn't acknowledge, the next one hears about it.

Handoff notes

A structured note at every shift change, with open incidents carried over automatically. The next person starts with context, not guesswork.

Calendar sync

Shifts appear in Google Calendar, Outlook or any iCal client, and update the moment a swap is approved.

Fairness report

See on-call hours per person, including nights and weekends, so the load stays even across the team.

FAQ

Questions teams ask before switching

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team and a real person will reply within one business day.

Can I import my existing schedule from a spreadsheet?
Yes. Upload a CSV or paste straight from Google Sheets or Excel, and Rotaline maps the columns to people and shifts. You'll see a preview of the rotation before anything goes live.
What happens if nobody acknowledges an alert?
The escalation policy moves to its next step, for example the secondary on-call and then the team lead, by push, SMS and phone call. If every step is exhausted, the policy repeats and the alert is flagged in the next handoff note.
Do you charge for SMS and phone calls?
No. SMS and phone alerts are included in the Team and Business plans, with no per-message fees. A generous fair-use limit applies, and we'll contact you well before you get near it.
Can contractors or people outside my company be on a rotation?
Yes. Invite anyone by email as a guest. Guests count as users on your plan, and you choose exactly which rotations and services they can see.
How does the free trial work?
You get every Business feature for 14 days, with no credit card required. When the trial ends, pick a plan or move to Free automatically. Your schedules and handoff notes are kept either way.
