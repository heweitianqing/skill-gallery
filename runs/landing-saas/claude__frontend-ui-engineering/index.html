Features

Everything a rotation needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for teams of 5 to 50 who have outgrown the shared spreadsheet and the "who's got tonight?" thread, but don't need a full incident-management suite.

  • Rotation builder

    Set up daily, weekly or follow-the-sun rotations across time zones. Rotaline handles daylight saving changes so nobody inherits a surprise 3 a.m. shift.

  • Swaps and overrides

    Request a swap in two clicks. The team lead approves it straight from email or chat, and the schedule updates for everyone.

  • Escalation policies

    Multi-step escalation by push notification, SMS and phone call. If the first responder doesn't acknowledge, the next step fires on your timer.

  • Handoff notes

    A structured note at every shift change, with open incidents carried over automatically. The next person starts with context instead of a scroll through chat.

  • Calendar sync

    Shifts appear in Google Calendar, Outlook or any iCal client, and update the moment a swap is approved.

  • Fairness report

    On-call hours per person, including nights and weekends, so load stays even and nobody quietly carries the team.

FAQ

Questions teams ask before switching.

Something else? Talk to the team

Can I import my existing schedule from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV or paste straight from Google Sheets or Excel, and Rotaline maps names, dates and time zones into a rotation you can review before publishing.

What happens if nobody acknowledges an alert?

The escalation policy moves to its next step, for example an SMS to the secondary and then a phone call to the team lead. You set the delay between steps, and every escalation is recorded in the next handoff note.

Do you charge for SMS and phone calls?

No. SMS and phone alerts are included in the Team and Business per-user price, with no per-message or per-minute fees.

Can contractors or people outside my company be on a rotation?

Yes. Invite anyone by email as a responder and limit them to the rotations they belong to. On paid plans they count as a regular user.

How does the free trial work?

You get every Business feature for 14 days with no credit card. When the trial ends, choose a plan or drop to Free automatically; your schedules and handoff history stay intact.
